# Privacy Policy

This policy covers Matthew Sonshine Moore’s portfolio website. Applications with different data practices require their own privacy notice; this policy does not describe an application's access to Google account data.

## Information collected

This is a static informational website. It does not provide user accounts, request access to your Google account, or include advertising or analytics scripts added by Matthew.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may collect technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, for security and operation of its service. See [GitHub’s Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you contact Matthew, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your inquiry. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive information.

## Use, sharing, and retention

Matthew does not sell personal information or use information from this site for targeted advertising. Messages may be processed by the communication provider you use and retained as needed to handle your inquiry or meet legal obligations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Hosting and communication providers manage their own records under their respective policies.

## Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent to Matthew, subject to applicable legal obligations. Contact Matthew through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/majjhima/) to arrange a private communication channel. Do not post personal information in public GitHub issues.

## External links

Linked websites and services operate under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover their practices.

## Changes and contact

Updates will be posted on this page. For privacy questions, contact [Matthew on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/majjhima/).
